Mathematical Framework & Pressure Formulation

1. Situational Leverage Index (LI): Pressure is strictly evaluated as a property of match context before delivery: LI(s) = Σ P(o) · |WP(s_o) - WP(s)|. Tiers isolate situations where LI exceeds 1.5x (Tier 1) and 3.0x (Tier 2) league averages, capturing death-phase chases and top-order wicket collapses.

2. Contextual Runs Added (y): Measures projected runs above expectation for each delivery. Batters earn positive credit (+y) for boundary-striking and dot avoidance; bowlers earn positive credit (-runs + wickets) for economy and breakthrough wickets under pressure.

3. Hierarchical Empirical Bayes Shrinkage: Small pressure sample noise is filtered via ridge regularization: Level = Skill + κ · Δ_pressure (penalty κ = 0.5). Players are ranked on their Lower Confidence Bound (Composite LCB = Composite - 1.0 · SE), ensuring both elite ceiling and proven sample depth.

4. Rajasthan Royals Auction Fit: Recommendations directly address Jaipur's Sawai Mansingh Stadium dynamics—prioritizing domestic finishing engines (Rinku Singh, Ashutosh Sharma) and high-velocity death pace / mystery wrist-spin (Lockie Ferguson, Sufiyan Muqeem).
